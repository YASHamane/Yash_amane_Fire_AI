# Income-tax rules for a salaried resident individual: FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)

Scope: salary income only, resident individual. All amounts in whole rupees.
Source tags like [S1], [S10] refer to `rules_data/sources.md` (official Income Tax Department and Finance Act 2025 publications).
This text is the law input for FY 2025-26.

## 1. Summary of Changes in Finance Act 2025 [S10]
- Old tax regime: No changes to slab rates, surcharge, cess, 87A rebate, or deductions.
- Standard deduction on salary: Remains Rs 50,000 (old) and Rs 75,000 (new) [S2, S3].
- New tax regime slabs: Widened with basic exemption increased to Rs 4,00,000 and 7 progressive tiers.
- Section 87A rebate (new regime): Limit increased to Rs 12,00,000 with maximum rebate of Rs 60,000. Marginal relief applies for income exceeding Rs 12,00,000.

## 2. Rounding [S9]
- Section 288A: total income is rounded to the nearest multiple of Rs 10 before tax is computed.
- Section 288B: the amount payable is rounded to the nearest multiple of Rs 10.
- Paise are dropped first, then 5+ rounds up, 4- rounds down.

## 3. Standard deduction on salary, section 16(ia) [S2, S3]
- Old regime: Rs 50,000, or salary if lower.
- New regime: Rs 75,000, or salary if lower.

## 4. Old regime slabs (unchanged) [S1]
- Below 60 years: Up to 2,50,000 nil; 2,50,001 to 5,00,000: 5%; 5,00,001 to 10,00,000: 20%; above 10,00,000: 30%.
- 60 to 79 years: Up to 3,00,000 nil; 3,00,001 to 5,00,000: 5%; 5,00,001 to 10,00,000: 20%; above 10,00,000: 30%.
- 80 years or more: Up to 5,00,000 nil; 5,00,001 to 10,00,000: 20%; above 10,00,000: 30%.

## 5. New regime slabs for FY 2025-26, section 115BAC(1A) [S10]
Same for all age bands:
- up to 4,00,000: nil
- 4,00,001 to 8,00,000: 5%
- 8,00,001 to 12,00,000: 10%
- 12,00,001 to 16,00,000: 15%
- 16,00,001 to 20,00,000: 20%
- 20,00,001 to 24,00,000: 25%
- above 24,00,000: 30%

## 6. Deductions (unchanged)
- Old regime: 80C up to 1,50,000; 80CCD(1B) up to 50,000; 80D self 25,000 (50,000 senior); 80D parents 25,000 (50,000 senior); home loan interest self-occupied 24(b) up to 2,00,000.
- New regime: none of the above allowed.

## 7. House Rent Allowance exemption (unchanged, old regime only) [S8]
Least of actual HRA received, rent minus 10% of salary, and 50% (metro) / 40% (non-metro) of salary.

## 8. Rebate under section 87A [S7, S10]
- Old regime: Total income <= 5,00,000 -> rebate = lesser of tax and 12,500. No marginal relief.
- New regime:
  - If total income <= 12,00,000: rebate = lesser of tax and 60,000 (zero tax payable).
  - If total income > 12,00,000 and tax > (total income - 12,00,000): rebate = tax - (total income - 12,00,000) (marginal relief).

## 9. Surcharge and marginal relief (unchanged) [S1]
Old regime: 10% (>50L), 15% (>1Cr), 25% (>2Cr), 37% (>5Cr).
New regime: 10% (>50L), 15% (>1Cr), 25% (>2Cr).

## 10. Health and education cess (unchanged) [S1]
4% of (tax after rebate + surcharge).
